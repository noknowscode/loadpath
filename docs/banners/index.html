Loadpath banners

Gallery banners for the Loadpath sample (a 2D truss workbench that solves as you draw), composed from real Release WebAssembly captures of the app at 2x and 3x. Every final is 1200 × 630 at 1x and 2400 × 1260 at 2x; the two strongest also come as a 1920 × 1080 hero. Sources and capture details are in README.md.
